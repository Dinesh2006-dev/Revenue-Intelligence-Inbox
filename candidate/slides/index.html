Dataset Structure & Scope
  • Total Ingested Records: Exactly 44 unique records (30 calls, 14 calendar meetings).
  • Initial Delivery (Rev 1): 17 usable conversation texts (10 calls, 7 meetings).
  • Update Delivery (Rev 2): M07 delivers 27 turns, expanding coverage to 18 (40.9%).

Authoritative Metric Definitions
  • Transcript Coverage: 18 / 44 = 40.9% (Usable texts divided by total applied records).
  • Source-Reported Connected Rate: 11 / 30 = 36.7% (Calls where is_connected=true divided by 30 non-null calls, 0 unknown).
  • Separation of Concepts: Stored upstream connection classification is explicitly isolated from transcript-derived findings.
  • Null Preservation: Null strictly means value absent; never coerced into false, zero, or empty.
Deduplication Contract
  • Logical Identity: Strictly the composite key (source_type, source_id).
  • Anti-Pattern Defense: Never deduplicate on title, company name, phone token, or array index.
  • Revision Precedence: Higher revision replaces full payload; older revision is ignored.
  • Override Invariance: Manual routing decisions are preserved across subsequent re-imports and restarts.

Difficult Routing Case & Deliberately Rejected Decision

Architectural Trade-offs, Edge-Case Governance, and Honest Uncertainty

Difficult Case: M04 (Cedarwave Distribution)
  • Tension: Existing customer experiencing in-store prepaid card declines.
  • Conflicting Intents: Customer demands credit card integration for their INR 1.3 crore bank facility; rep pitches prepaid migration.
  • Hierarchy Friction: Customer also reports confused department structure in admin view.
  • Resolution: Avoided naive 'Sales Opportunity' route. Classified as customer_concern / Retention Risk with action item on bank card statement feed.
Deliberately Rejected: Synthetic Pipeline Valuation
  • Alternative Considered: Extracting spoken currency numbers into an aggregate CRM pipeline total.
  • Why Rejected: Numbers mentioned represent customer operational costs (M03 INR 10-13L travel pool, M04 INR 1.3 crore credit limit, M06 INR 1.3L invoice)—NOT SpendNest contract values!
  • Unacceptable Risk: Summing these numbers manufactures a deceptive multi-crore pipeline metric.
  • Accepted Trade-off: Surfaced qualitative deal signals and confirmed next steps without fabricated financial totals.
Rule-Based Determinism vs Live LLM Calls
  • Zero Cloud Cost: Runs entirely client-side without API keys, token limits, or network latency.
  • Auditable Reasoning: Every routing suggestion is backed by transparent deterministic checks.
  • Offline Governance: Sensitive conversation text never leaves the client browser.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Override: Managers retain 100% control to adjust routes with immutable audit logs.

Known Limitations & Boundaries

  • Background Call Visibility: 20 calls lack transcripts due to delivery redaction; classified strictly on metadata.
  • No Authoritative Staff Directory: Rep roles are inferred from call direction and meeting titles.
